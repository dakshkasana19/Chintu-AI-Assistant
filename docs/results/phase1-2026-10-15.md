# Phase 1 Results — 2026-10-15

## Implemented

- One persistent `sounddevice.InputStream`, mono float32 audio at 16 kHz, fixed-size frames (default 30 ms).
- Bounded frame queue drops the oldest queued frame on overload; microphone callback failures and inactive streams surface as explicit capture errors.
- Runtime retries after microphone start/callback/device-stop errors using configurable recovery delay.
- Silero VAD ONNX CPU backend with a pinned model SHA-256 and 512-sample model window; WebRTC VAD fallback when the model is missing or fails to load.
- VAD consecutive-speech onset confirmation, configurable pre-roll (default 300 ms), end-silence hangover (default 700 ms), maximum utterance duration (default 15 s), and utterance RMS metadata.
- Real microphone RMS signal feeds the overlay while listening.
- Model download supports HTTP resume and verifies SHA-256 before atomically promoting the file.

## Limitations

The runtime captures speech segments but has no wake-word detector or ASR consumer yet; captured segments are logged, not interpreted. It also has no TTS, so microphone muting during speech output and barge-in cancellation cannot yet be exercised. The tests use synthetic frames and mocks; they do not depend on physical audio hardware.
